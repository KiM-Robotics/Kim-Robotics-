### Who We Are

**KiM Robotics** is an educational robotics and technology company focused on developing practical robotic platforms, providing hands-on technical training, and supporting research in robotics, embedded systems, and automation.

We bridge the gap between theoretical engineering education and real-world implementation through open learning platforms and applied robotics solutions.

---

### What We Do

- **Educational Robotics Platforms**  
  Hands-on robot kits designed for learning, experimentation, and skill development.

- **Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMR)**  
  Development of differential drive and mecanum-based mobile robot platforms.

- **ROS 2 Development**  
  Training and development support for Robot Operating System 2 applications.

- **Workshops & Technical Training**  
  Practical sessions for students, educators, and institutions.

- **Research & Prototyping Support**  
  Assistance for academic and industrial robotics projects.
